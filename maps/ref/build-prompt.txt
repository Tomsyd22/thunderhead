4. TERRAIN CONSTRUCTION AND PRECEDENCE

Mean sea level is height 0 m. Deep ocean floor defaults to -20 m; use specified channel/fjord bed heights where supplied. Build a shoreline transition with shallow sand/rock shelf before deep water. Water appearance must reflect actual movement/depth classes.

Geometry precedence, applied deterministically:
1. Construct the land mask: polygon, full-square, or union of ellipses.
2. Subtract water_cuts, then restore land_additions such as the fjord island.
3. Set base_ground_m on land; apply shoreline blending inward.
4. Add hills and quarry depressions.
5. Flatten base pads and landmark pads; grade their perimeter.
6. Grade roads/ramps and bridge approaches. Water cuts remain water; only explicit bridges may cross deep channels.
7. Reserve resource service areas, dock access and landing beaches.
8. Add minor terrain noise and environmental assets only outside protected masks.

Ellipse land islands must receive an organic coastline treatment constrained within a 6 m displacement of the mathematical boundary. Keep logical navigation and rendered shoreline consistent. Do not displace narrow channels into closure. The exact mask is the source layout; irregular edges are surface detail.

For a hill, compute d²=((x-cx)/rx)²+((y-cy)/ry)² in q coordinates. Within d<1, use weight=(1-d²)². Raise toward peak_height_m using that weight, rather than adding the full peak to the existing height. Use the maximum overlapping hill contribution. Peaks are absolute elevations above water, not extra heights.

Roads follow the specified centerline polylines. Use rounded joins/fillets of at least 20 m radius where space permits; preserve each supplied waypoint within 8 m. Grade their surface to a maximum longitudinal slope of 6 percent and cross slope of 2 percent. A 24 m rise requires at least 400 m of ramp at 6 percent. If a fixed land connection cannot satisfy the slope limit, lower the nearby ridge or spread the grading corridor; do not silently insert an impassable ramp. Report any unavoidable layout conflict before relocating authoritative points.

Use fine terrain variation of no more than ±0.35 m outside protected areas. Construction pads, doorways, road surfaces and resource unloading areas require flat/graded surfaces without random noise. Cliff meshes and collision must match; visually clear slopes above the vehicle traversal threshold are impassable.

Quarries use the specified floor_height_m and two entry points. Give each entrance a 16 m clear ramp with maximum 6 percent slope. Do not create a resource pit with only one exit.
