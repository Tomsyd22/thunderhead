## 11. Faction 2: The Compact

**Doctrine.** Heavy, slow, cheap infantry, expensive armour. Best tank at every tier, best artillery, the only trench. Infantry dig in (uncrushable, +30% damage) and Rocket Troopers brace. Veterancy comes 20% faster and the Arsenal makes units start Veteran. The Thunderhead Strike is the only true nuke in the game.

**Build system.** RA2 classic: queue in the sidebar, place inside the radius.

**Look and sound.** Olive drab and gunmetal, red stripes, stencilled numbers, concrete everything. Announcer is a gravel-voiced field marshal. Music: brass-heavy orchestral march with electric guitar.

**Weak to:** anything faster than it. Helix bikes and Shades raid Harvesters and Hailstorms; Lattice swarms overwhelm single big units; Netrunners turn Behemoths around. The Stratofortress dies to any AA that gets to shoot it for ten seconds.
