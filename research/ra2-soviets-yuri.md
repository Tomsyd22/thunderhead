# Red Alert 2 / Yuri's Revenge — Soviet & Yuri Game-Design Reference

Scope: Soviet faction (RA2 + YR changes) and Yuri faction (YR). Allies and core mechanics (economy, damage model, veterancy formula) are covered elsewhere.

**Data provenance.** Unless marked otherwise, every number below is read directly from the shipped `rulesmd.ini` (Yuri's Revenge, patch 1.001 — confirmed by Slave Miner = 1750 and Chaos Drone = 1000, both post-patch values) and `rules.ini` (Red Alert 2) as distributed in two independent GitHub mirrors (moshowgame/ra2yuri "原版" originals; DeathFishAtEase/YRModdingBase standardised copy). Both YR copies were diffed on key sections and agree. Wiki/manual values are cited where rules values do not exist (e.g. voice tone, hard-coded behaviour). Anything I could not confirm is tagged **(unverified)**.

**Reading the tables.**
- *HP* = `Strength`. *Speed* = `Speed` (leptons-based abstract movement rating; higher is faster). *Range* in cells. *ROF* = frames between shots (game logic runs 15 frames/s at normal speed, so ROF 60 = one shot every 4 s).
- *Verses* lists damage multipliers against the 11 armour classes in this fixed order: `none, flak, plate, light, medium, heavy, wood, steel, concrete, special_1, special_2`. Infantry use none/flak/plate; vehicles light/medium/heavy; buildings wood/steel/concrete; special_1 = Terror Drone, special_2 = spawned missiles.
- *Vet/Elite* abilities from `VeteranAbilities`/`EliteAbilities`: STRONGER (armour ×1.5 effective), FIREPOWER (×1.1), ROF (delay ×0.6), SIGHT, FASTER (×1.2), SELF_HEAL, SCATTER. `ElitePrimary` weapon swaps are listed per unit. Veteran requires destroying 3× own value (`VeteranRatio=3.0`), cap 2 levels.
- *Prereq codes*: NACNST ConYard, NAPOWR Tesla Reactor, NAREFN Refinery (PROC = any refinery), NAHAND Barracks, NAWEAP War Factory, NARADR Radar (RADAR = any radar), NATECH Battle Lab (TECH = any lab), NAYARD Shipyard, NADEPT Service Depot; Yuri: YACNST, YAPOWR, YABRCK, YAWEAP, NAPSIS Psychic Radar, YATECH, YAYARD, YAGRND.
- *Soylent* = credits refunded by the Yuri Grinder (and credit value for Cloning/Bio logic). Vehicles: Soylent = Cost (100%). Infantry: Soylent = Cost/2 (50%). Exceptions noted.
- Voice descriptions are tone-only (no scripted lines transcribed). Voice categories follow the rules keys that exist on each unit: Select, Move, Attack, Feedback (under fire / fear), Die, plus specials (Deploy, SecondaryAttack, Capture/Enter, Crashing).

## G. SOVIET DOCTRINE & FLAVOUR

**Visual identity.** Default faction colour dark red (`Color=DarkRed` on all four Soviet countries). Heavy, riveted industrial brutalism: bulbous Tesla Reactor coils, Soviet star and hammer-and-sickle iconography, concrete-and-rust War Factory with crane gantries, red-tiled Barracks, onion-dome Radar/Battle Lab motifs, the bulging Nuclear Reactor with cooling towers. Units are squat and oversized (Rhino, Apocalypse with twin barrels and missile racks, the zeppelin Kirov with a shark-mouth nose). Tesla-blue lightning and sickly green radiation (`RadColor=0,255,0`) are the two signature VFX. Iron Curtain tints units a deep red (`IronCurtainColor=0` → ColorAdd red).

**Audio/music.** Frank Klepacki's industrial-metal score: RA2 — "Hell March 2" (the Soviet anthem, marching boots + distorted guitar), "Industrofunk", "Ready the Army", "Grinder", "In Deep", "Motorized", "Power", "200 Meters", "Destroy", "Burn", "Probing", "Blow It Up", "Eagle Hunter", "Fortification", "Jank", "C&C in the House"; YR adds "Brain Freeze", "Trance L. Vania", "Bully Kit", "Deceiver", "Defend the Base", "Drok", "Phat Attack", "Tactics", "Score", "Yuri Teaser Remix". Soviet EVA is a stern female voice; unit voices are uniformly Russian-accented (Cuban/Arab for country specials), ranging from frightened conscripts to boastful heroes.

**Strengths.** Highest raw HP and alpha damage per credit (Rhino 400 HP/90 dmg for 900; Apocalypse 800 HP); cheapest infantry (Conscript 100) and garrison tool (Battle Bunker); War Miner is armed, self-healing, double-capacity; Tesla Coil is the strongest single-target defence and works unpowered when charged; Kirov is the only unit that can ignore ground defences entirely; Iron Curtain enables suicide and MCV-walk plays; Nuclear Reactor gives +2000 power from one 1000-credit footprint; Industrial Plant (YR) makes every vehicle 25% cheaper; deep naval roster (subs beat Allied surface fleet cheaply, Dreadnought range 25).

**Weaknesses.** Slow (Rhino 6, Apoc 4, V3 4, Kirov 5); no fast infantry transport except Flak Track; poor long-range anti-infantry until Desolator/Siege Chopper; no air force proper (no Harriers), no spy, no repair IFV; everything is vulnerable to mind control except Miner/Drone/Boris/Dog; Tesla defences are power-hungry (−75) and die to one Prism/V3 volley; Nuclear Reactor is a liability; Battle Lab has only 500 HP; naval units are underwater and need radar/sensors.

**Standard competitive strategies (RA2/YR ladder conventions).**
1. *Rhino spam / tank-flood*: 2 refineries → War Factory, constant Rhinos, Service Depot for MCV-less repairs; Rhinos beat Grizzlies/Lashers head-on. Add Terror Drones to pick off Chrono Miners and IFVs.
2. *Conscript + Flak Track garrison pushes*: fill civilian buildings and Battle Bunkers near the enemy expansion; Flak Tracks deliver and give AA.
3. *Drone harassment*: 500-credit drones kill 1400-credit miners; forces Allied Pillbox/IFV spending.
4. *Iron Curtain + Demo Truck (Libya) or Curtain + Rhinos into base*: 50 s invulnerability window.
5. *Kirov late game* vs players without mobile AA; Apocalypse + Tesla Tank (Russia) deathball vs Allied tanks.
6. *Desolator (Iraq) area denial*: deployed Desolators behind walls stop GI/Guardian GI pushes and slave mining; Iraq is the consensus strongest Soviet country in YR because Desolators hard-counter Yuri's infantry economy.
7. *V3/Siege Chopper* to crack Prism Towers and Psychic Towers from outside range.
8. Vs Yuri: never attack with few expensive units (Mastermind/Towers steal them); use Conscript masses, Desolators, Terror Drones (immune), Kirovs are a trap (mind-controllable) — bring Flak.

## L. YURI MECHANICS

**Mind control (engine rules, rulesmd + ModEnc).**
- A `MindControl=yes` warhead that *hits* a non-immune TechnoType transfers ownership to the firer's house. Weapon `Damage` on the **Primary** weapon = how many objects the controller holds at once (Yuri Clone/Prime 1, Mastermind 3, Psychic Tower 3). Controlled units show the MINDANIM swirl; permanently controlled ones (Dominator) show MINDANIMR.
- **Temporary control**: tied to the controller. Controller dies / sells / is destroyed → all its captives revert to the original owner. Yuri Clone retargeting releases the previous captive. Psychic Tower loses power → releases.
- **Permanent control**: Psychic Dominator captures are not linked to any controller and never revert. Also, controlled infantry put into a building/garrison effectively become "permanent" while inside.
- **Immunity**: anything with `ImmuneToPsionics=yes` cannot be targeted. Vanilla buildable list: Attack Dogs (all), Yuri Clone, Psi Commando, Yuri Prime, Tanya, Boris, Brute, War Miner, Chrono Miner, Slave Miner, Slaves, Terror Drone, Chaos Drone, Giant Squid, Mastermind, Robot Tank, Intruder (Harrier), Black Eagle; plus all spawned missiles and (by default) all buildings except where set `no`. Aircraft in flight cannot be controlled regardless. Buildings default immune; Yuri Prime's `ControllerBuilding` warhead has 100% vs wood/steel/concrete so he can take any building whose section does not set `ImmuneToPsionics=yes` — Psychic Tower, Slave Miner, Rocket Pad are explicitly immune; most production/defence structures are `ImmuneToPsionics=no` and thus capturable. Psychic Dominator is hard-coded never to capture buildings.
- Controlled units keep their veterancy; the owner cannot build from captured factories' tech unless a ConYard of that side is also held (standard tech rules). Grinder/Bio Reactor accept controlled units and this permanently breaks the link (counts as consumed).
- **Mastermind overload** (`[SpecialWeapons]`): `OverloadCount=3,6,10,50`, `OverloadDamage=0,50,100,500`, `OverloadFrames=30,60,60,60`. Holding 4–6 → 50 dmg/4 s; 7–10 → 100/4 s; ≥11 → 500/4 s. With 500 HP and SelfHealing it survives a 4–6 overload for ~40 s, 7–10 for ~20 s.

**Grinder refund.** Credits = unit's `Soylent`. Vehicles: Soylent = full cost (Rhino 900, Apocalypse 1750, Slave Miner 1750, MCV 3000). Infantry: half cost (Initiate 100, Brute 250, Yuri Clone 400, Boris 750). Special: Chrono Ivan 500, Slaves 0, Yuri Dog 50. With Industrial Plant, vehicles built at 75% grind for 100% → +25% profit loop (e.g. Lasher built 525, ground 700). Mind-controlled enemy vehicles grind at full enemy cost — a Mastermind in a tank battle is literally cash. Transports grind their passengers (1.001).

**Bio Reactor infantry input.** `InfantryAbsorb=yes`, 5 slots, `ExtraPower=100` each: 150 → 650 power for 600 credits (vs Soviet Nuclear Reactor 2000 for 1000 + Battle Lab). Any infantry works: Slaves, Initiates (200 cr for +100 power), freed slaves, mind-controlled enemy infantry (frees the controller to grab another). Infantry inside do not heal/level; can be ejected at will; destruction releases them (controlled ones revert). The reactor's own HP (700) is the risk — spies also sabotage it like any power plant.

**Genetic Mutator (Brute conversion).** Superweapon `GeneticConverterSpecial`: recharge 5 min, `Range=5` cells, uses `MutateExplosion` warhead (CellSpread 5, 100% vs none/flak/plate infantry, 0% everything else, `InfDeath=9` = GENDEATH anim → `MakeInfantry` Brute). Every ground infantry in the circle — friend or foe, including Slaves, GIs, Boris, Tanya — dies and spawns a **Brute owned by the Mutator's owner** (friendly Initiates become 500-credit Brutes for 200). Dogs, Squids, Dolphins die without converting. Garrisoned infantry, passengers and Rocketeers (airborne) are protected. Spies can reset the timer.

**Psychic Dominator.** `PsychicDominatorSpecial`: recharge 10 min, targeting `Range=1.4`. Fires at `DominatorFireAtPercentage=20` into the head animation: (1) damage pulse `DominatorDamage=1000` with `DominatorWH` CellSpread 7, PercentAtMax 0.2, Verses 0% vs all units, **100% wood, 100% steel, 6% concrete** — wipes power plants, barracks, defences in ~7 cells, barely scratches ConYards (60 dmg) and does nothing to units; (2) **permanent** mind control of every non-immune unit within `DominatorCaptureRange=1` cell (3×3 footprint) — buildings never captured. Iron Curtain/Force Shield protect units/buildings from the damage; Curtained units are still capturable? (`ImmuneToPsionics` is the only check listed — **unverified**). Hard counters: spread armies, keep immune units, Spy the Dominator to reset.

**Chaos gas (berserk).** Chaos Drone's `PsychGasCreate` warhead (`Psychedelic=yes`, CellSpread 3, 600 base). Berserk duration in frames = damage after Verses/armour (infantry 600 f = 40 s; light/medium/heavy vehicles 50% → 300 f = 20 s). Berserk units ignore orders, attack nearest unit (own side included) and are tinted (`BerserkColor=4`). Immune: `ImmuneToPsionics=yes` units and all units owned by the gas's firer. Re-exposure refreshes. The lingering CDGAS cloud animation lasts ~450-frame cycle (art `Rate=450`). Buildings unaffected.

**Virus toxin cloud.** Kill → `InfDeath=8` → VIRUSD anim spawns 3 `VirusCloud1` particle streams (`BehavesLike=Gas`, `Damage=5`, warhead VirusGas CellSpread 1; 100% infantry, 50% vehicles light/med/heavy, 0% buildings), decaying through `VirusCloudD1` (5 dmg). Cloud ticks repeatedly for several seconds **(exact tick count unverified; MaxEC/EndStateAI govern lifetime)**. Chain-kills: each infantry death in gas spawns another cloud. Virus herself `ImmuneToPoison`. Vehicles standing in gas take chip damage; Terror Drones/Chaos Drones are vehicles (50%).

## M. YURI DOCTRINE & FLAVOUR

**Visual identity.** Faction colour purple (`Color=DarkRed` is only the country stub; UI/sidebar, Yuri's units' house colour default and marketing are violet/magenta; laser inner colour `216,0,184`). Architecture: bulbous, organic, biomechanical — brass-and-bone domes, ribbed cylinders, glowing green psychic crystals, the giant brain-in-a-dome Psychic Dominator and the eyeball-studded Psychic Tower; Bio Reactor tubes with infantry visibly suspended in green fluid; Grinder with its toothed maw; Gattling Cannon on a skeletal tripod. Units: shaved-headed Initiates in black-and-purple robes, hunched grey Brutes, the floating saucer Disc, the three-wheeled Lasher and tripod Gattling Tank, the Mastermind brain tank, Magnetron's spinning dish. Mind-controlled targets glow with the swirling MINDANIM. Infantry death anims are grotesque: head-pop (`InfantryHeadPop=YURIDIE`), viral swelling (VIRUSD), mutation (GENDEATH), brute-splat (BRUTDIE).

**Audio/music.** Shares Klepacki's YR score; "Brain Freeze", "Drok", "Deceiver" and "Trance L. Vania" lean trance/industrial-electronic to match the psychic theme. Yuri EVA is a sinister male voice (Yuri himself in cutscenes). Unit voices are universally sly, whispering, or cult-ecstatic; several (Disc, Mastermind, Slave Miner) are darkly comic.

**Strengths.** Converts the enemy's economy into yours (mind control + Grinder); best early infantry (Initiate) and garrison fighter; Gattling line gives cheap, scalable AA/anti-infantry; Slave Miner starts mining before Soviets have a refinery; Bio Reactor power is cheap and infantry-fed; Floating Disc shuts down a whole base's power; Magnetron neutralises heavy tanks; Psychic Tower (3 units each) makes static defence punish small raids; Brute/Virus answer both infantry and tank spam cheaply; immune roster (Brutes, drones, Masterminds) fights other Yuris well.

**Weaknesses.** Lasher is the weakest MBT; no Service Depot (unit repair only via Engineers/captured depots), no dog (spy detection only via Yuri Clone/Psychic Radar — spies hit Slave Miners hard), no AA ship, no artillery that hits infantry; everything key is fragile (Battle Lab 500, Psychic Tower 455, Magnetron 150, Virus 100); heavy dependence on power (towers/Gattling/Radar) → vulnerable to Spies, Discs, Chrono Legionnaires; Slaves die to radiation/snipers; Mastermind self-destructs if misused; mind control does nothing vs Brutes/Robot Tanks/Terror Drones/dogs; Dominator barely scratches concrete.

**Typical play.** Open Slave Miner + Barracks → Initiates in garrisons; second Slave Miner; War Factory → Gattling Tanks for AA/anti-infantry + Lashers in Tank Bunkers; Psychic Radar → Yuri Clones to steal miners/tanks, Virus vs infantry; Battle Lab → Masterminds behind Gattling screens, Floating Discs to black out power then hit with Magnetrons; Grinder everything stolen. Community balance consensus: Yuri is strongest in 1v1 vs Allies, hard-countered by Iraq Desolators and Soviet Terror Drone/Conscript floods; Yuri vs Yuri devolves into Brute + Gattling + Magnetron.
